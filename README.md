# Child Benefit Calculator UK — And the High Income Charge

> Child Benefit is £27.05 a week for the eldest child and £17.9 for each other, clawed back between £60,000 and £80,000.

Reference notes for the UK tax year **2026/27**, computed from HMRC's
published rates rather than copied from another site.

## How it works

| Children | A year |
|---|---:|
| 1 | £1,406.60 |
| 2 | £2,337.40 |
| 3 | £3,268.20 |

The High Income Child Benefit Charge takes it back at 1% of the benefit for every £200 of adjusted net income above £60,000, so it is fully clawed back by £80,000.

For a family with three children that creates an effective marginal rate of around 60% across that band.

**Claim it even if you will lose it all.** The claim is what credits your National Insurance record toward the State Pension while you are caring for a child under 12. You can claim and opt out of payments.

## What this does not cover

The charge is assessed on the **higher earner's** adjusted net income, not household income. Two parents on £59,000 each keep everything; one on £80,000 loses all of it.

## Source and tax year

Figures are for the 2026/27 tax year, which runs 6 April 2026
to 5 April 2027, and were checked against
[gov.uk](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/rates-and-thresholds-for-employers-2026-to-2027) on 28 August 2026.

Rates change every April, and often at a Budget. Check the tax year a figure
belongs to before relying on it.
